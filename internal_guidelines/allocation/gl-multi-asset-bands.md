# Global Multi-Asset Allocation Bands

Internal investment guidance setting strategic weights and tolerance bands for global multi-asset mandates. This is not a research note and not investment advice to any client.

Last revised 2026-03-04 by the Investment Policy Committee.

## B.1 Standing

These bands are the strategic default for a global balanced mandate. A weight outside its band requires approval under the discretion matrix at D.3.

As with the taxable fixed income guide, a band that rests on a research note is suspended if that note is superseded or withdrawn, and returns to the prior committee-adopted level pending review.

## B.2 Strategic Weights

Global equities forty-five percent, global fixed income thirty-five percent, real assets ten percent, private markets ten percent for eligible clients and reallocated pro rata across the liquid sleeves for clients who are not eligible.

## B.3 Tolerance Bands

Plus or minus five percentage points on equities and fixed income, plus or minus three on real assets. Private markets carries no tolerance band in the ordinary sense because the allocation cannot be traded; see B.6.

## B.4 Duration Underwriting

The diversification credit given to fixed income duration in setting the equity band is underwritten at the reduced level recommended by MA-GL-RATES-REGIME 2026-02 at R.3, rather than at the level implied by the post-2010 correlation sample.

The committee adopted this in March 2026 and it is the reason the equity band at B.3 is five points rather than the seven points in force previously.

## B.5 Regime Review

The committee has accepted the working assumption at R.1 of MA-GL-RATES-REGIME 2026-02 and will review the strategic weights at B.2 against it at the 2026 annual review. The weights at B.2 have not yet been changed for it.

## B.6 Private Markets Band

The private markets weight is managed through commitment pacing rather than rebalancing. A drift above the strategic weight caused by a denominator effect is not a breach and is not traded. A drift caused by over-commitment is a pacing failure and is escalated under D.4.

Unfunded commitments are subject to the liquidity constraint at V.6 of MA-GL-PRIVATE-MARKETS 2025-12, which is binding as a limit.

## B.7 Review

Reviewed annually, and out of cycle whenever a research note these bands cite is re-issued or withdrawn.
