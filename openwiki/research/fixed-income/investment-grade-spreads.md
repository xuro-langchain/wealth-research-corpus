## Research conclusion — FI-US-IG-SPREADS 2025-09

**Edition 2025-09** was published by US Fixed Income Research on 2025-09-18 and applies to positions taken on or after 2025-10-01. The desk is **underweight US investment-grade corporate credit** because spreads sit inside the tenth percentile of their 20-year range and offer limited compensation for cyclical risk. This is a valuation view, not a forecast of imminent credit deterioration. [Source: IG Spreads, G.1](repo://research/FI/US/IG-SPREADS/2025-09.md#L1-L7)

The valuation signal remains after relevant index-quality adjustments: option-adjusted spreads are near their tightest level since 2007, and accounting for the index's higher average rating and shorter average duration reduces but does not remove the perceived richness. At the same time, index-level leverage is stable and interest coverage is comfortable. Those fundamentals are why the conclusion should not be interpreted as a broad balance-sheet stress call. [Sources: IG Spreads, G.2–G.3](repo://research/FI/US/IG-SPREADS/2025-09.md#L9-L15)

## Expression and funding role

The research note calls for an underweight of **approximately four percentage points within the taxable fixed-income sleeve**. Its proceeds are intended to fund a higher-conviction allocation elsewhere in that sleeve, **not** to become a standalone cash position. [Source: IG Spreads, G.5](repo://research/FI/US/IG-SPREADS/2025-09.md#L21-L23)

The allocation guide supplies the separate, binding implementation decision: investment-grade corporate credit has a 28% target versus 32% neutral, a four-point underweight. It explicitly pairs that underweight with the top-bracket municipal allocation's 22% target versus 18% neutral, making the corporate reduction the funding source for the municipal overweight. The guide—not the research note—sets these portfolio weights. [Sources: Allocation Guide, A.3 and A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L17-L23) [Allocation Guide, A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L33-L37)

This dependency is an implementation invariant: if the municipal overweight is suspended because its cited research is superseded or withdrawn, the corporate underweight is suspended too and both positions return to neutral. Retaining the corporate underweight after the municipal use of proceeds disappears would leave the sleeve structurally short credit without the guide's stated rationale. [Source: Allocation Guide, A.1 and A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L7-L11) [Allocation Guide, A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L33-L37)

## What could make the underweight costly

The principal challenge is technical, not a contradiction of the valuation observation: liability-driven-investor demand has been the dominant support for six quarters and shows no sign of reversal. A continuation beyond the desk's expectation can keep spreads tight. A growth acceleration that validates prevailing spreads is a second risk. Finally, the position is carry-negative, so a prolonged range-bound market imposes a carry cost even without spread widening. [Sources: IG Spreads, G.4 and G.6](repo://research/FI/US/IG-SPREADS/2025-09.md#L17-L19) [IG Spreads, G.6](repo://research/FI/US/IG-SPREADS/2025-09.md#L25-L27)

## Control lifecycle and operating boundary

A research recommendation is an input to a control, not authority for a portfolio manager to select a weight. The guide is reviewed quarterly and out of cycle when a cited research note is reissued or withdrawn. If a cited note is superseded or withdrawn, its derived weight is suspended rather than carried forward until the Investment Policy Committee re-adopts it against a replacement; the manager must escalate rather than directly adopt the replacement recommendation. [Sources: Allocation Guide, A.1 and A.8](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L7-L11) [Allocation Guide, A.8](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L49-L51) [Discretion Matrix, D.4](repo://guidelines/authority/discretion-matrix.md#L31-L37)

Ordinary drift is different from a research-change suspension. The 28% target normally has a ±2-percentage-point, month-end market-value tolerance band and drift beyond it is rebalanced in the next monthly cycle. A suspension-driven breach is not automatically rebalanced because the successor weight requires Committee action. [Source: Allocation Guide, A.6](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L39-L43)
