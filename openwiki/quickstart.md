## Start with the question, then keep the authorities separate

Use this page to route a proposed trade, allocation change, eligibility decision, or historical review. Research editions and regulatory bulletins are frozen authorities; internal guidance is revised in place. The edition in force on the position date remains that position’s basis of record, while current regulatory and living-guidance constraints govern an action now. [Corpus model](repo://README.md#L39-L48)

| Layer | What it answers | What it cannot do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Research conclusion** | The desk’s thesis, expression, assumptions, risks, and review trigger in a named edition | Set a binding account weight or clear an exception |
| **Binding internal guidance** | Operative targets, limits, bands, approvals, and documentation | Replace research or waive a regulatory requirement |
| **Regulatory overlay** | Effective-date-specific tax treatment or legal constraints | Select a portfolio allocation |
| **Basis of record** | Which edition explains a historical position and how to assemble the current decision | Carry a superseded view into a new binding weight |

## Municipal re-issue: minimum control sequence

1. **Select the edition by position date.** FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT 2025-06 applies to positions from 2025-07-01; FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT 2026-04 **supersedes** it for positions taken on or after 2026-05-01. The former edition remains the basis of record for positions taken while it stood. [Municipal 2025-06 notice and applicability](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2025-06.md#L3-L5) [Municipal 2025-06 publication](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2025-06.md#L16-L18) [Municipal 2026-04 publication and M.1](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2026-04.md#L16-L27)
2. **Apply the AMT overlay to the current action.** Revenue Procedure 2025-41 applies to taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01 and sets $500,000/$1,000,000 AMT exemption phase-out thresholds for unmarried/joint filers; they are not indexed before 2030. Specified private-activity-bond interest remains an AMT preference item, but qualified 501(c)(3) interest remains outside AMTI. [Revenue Procedure N.3–N.5](repo://bulletins/IRS/2025-08-rev-proc-2025-41-amt-thresholds.md#L18-L34)
3. **Read the replacement as research, not implementation.** The 2026-04 desk view is neutral, withdraws the broad private-activity concentration, retains a modest qualified-501(c)(3) preference, and recommends no new non-501(c)(3) private-activity positions without a forced-sale recommendation for existing positions. [Municipal 2026-04 M.1, M.3–M.4](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2026-04.md#L20-L24) [Municipal 2026-04 M.3–M.4](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2026-04.md#L55-L78)
4. **Apply the binding-guide response.** The live taxable guide says a weight derived from a superseded cited note is suspended rather than carried forward pending committee re-adoption. Its former 22% municipal target versus 18% neutral and the 28% IG-corporate target versus 32% neutral identify the historical committee implementation, not an automatically adopted 2026-04 allocation. [Allocation guide A.1, A.3, A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L7-L11) [Allocation guide A.3](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L17-L23) [Allocation guide A.5](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L33-L37)
5. **Suspend the paired trade and escalate.** A.5 returns the suspended municipal overweight and paired corporate underweight to their stated neutral references; a suspension-caused condition is not an ordinary drift rebalance. The manager records the superseded and replacement notes, derived weights, and affected accounts, then escalates to the committee without retaining the old weight or directly adopting the replacement research. [Allocation guide A.5–A.6](repo://guidelines/allocation/us-taxable-fixed-income.md#L33-L43) [Discretion matrix D.4](repo://guidelines/authority/discretion-matrix.md#L31-L37)
6. **Retain the cleared basis.** A cleared escalation records the condition, clearing authority, facts relied on, and date; without it, audit treats the position as unapproved. [Discretion matrix D.6](repo://guidelines/authority/discretion-matrix.md#L49-L51)

### Why the research changed

The historical 2025-06 overweight was an after-tax, top-bracket thesis whose load-bearing premise was that most such clients fell outside AMT; the note said a material threshold reduction would invalidate the recommendation. The 2026-04 note attributes withdrawal of the overweight to the new thresholds and does not re-justify its former size on the prior fundamental case. [Municipal 2025-06 M.2–M.3](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2025-06.md#L26-L40) [Municipal 2026-04 M.2](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2026-04.md#L29-L53)

The tax distinction is specific: the regulatory overlay retains the AMT preference treatment for specified private-activity bonds and the qualified-501(c)(3) exception. The replacement research consequently retains hospital and private-higher-education qualified-501(c)(3) preference and withdraws the predominantly non-501(c)(3) airport preference. [Revenue Procedure N.4–N.5](repo://bulletins/IRS/2025-08-rev-proc-2025-41-amt-thresholds.md#L24-L34) [Municipal 2026-04 M.3](repo://research/FI/US/MUNI-CREDIT/2026-04.md#L55-L67)

## General controls for other routes

Private-markets research applies only to eligible clients with a genuine ten-year horizon; eligibility is a regulatory determination rather than a research conclusion. An approval cannot clear an ineligible client or a stated regulatory breach. [Private-markets research V.1–V.2](repo://research/MA/GL/PRIVATE-MARKETS/2025-12.md#L16-L22) [Discretion matrix D.5](repo://guidelines/authority/discretion-matrix.md#L39-L47)

For a qualified-client determination on or after 2026-06-29, SEC Order IA-7104 sets adjusted Rule 205-3 paths. A pre-effective-date determination is preserved only for its original contract or investment; it cannot support a new entry on or after that date, and the order does not adjust accredited-investor or qualified-purchaser standards. [SEC Order Q.2, Q.4, Q.6](repo://bulletins/SEC/2026-04-order-ia-7104-qualified-client.md#L14-L18) [SEC Order Q.4](repo://bulletins/SEC/2026-04-order-ia-7104-qualified-client.md#L24-L28) [SEC Order Q.6](repo://bulletins/SEC/2026-04-order-ia-7104-qualified-client.md#L34-L36)

For any cleared escalation, retain the required basis; for private-markets eligibility, retain the pathway, evidence, decision maker, and date. [Discretion matrix D.6](repo://guidelines/authority/discretion-matrix.md#L49-L51) [Eligibility guide P.5](repo://guidelines/suitability/private-markets-eligibility.md#L27-L29)

When the answer is not clear, do not infer a binding weight from research or waive an overlay through approval. Escalate under the authority guidance and assemble the documented basis of record.
