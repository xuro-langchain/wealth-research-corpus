# SEC Order IA-6961 — Inflation Adjustment of the Qualified Client Dollar Amount Tests

Issued 2026-04. Effective 2026-06-29. Trimmed digest of a real published order, retained in this corpus for the provisions that bear on private markets eligibility. Full text: https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2026/ia-6955.pdf

## Q.1 Purpose

This order adjusts for inflation the dollar amount thresholds of the two tests by which a client qualifies as a qualified client under rule 205-3 of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940. Section 205(e) of the Act requires the Commission to make this adjustment every five years, rounded to the nearest $100,000, by reference to the Personal Consumption Expenditures Chain-Type Price Index. Prior adjustments were made in 2011, 2016 and 2021.

## Q.2 Adjusted Thresholds

Effective 2026-06-29, the assets-under-management test is raised from $1,100,000 to $1,400,000, and the net worth test is raised from $2,200,000 to $2,700,000.

A client is a qualified client if the client has at least $1,400,000 under management with the adviser immediately after entering into the advisory contract, or if the adviser reasonably believes, immediately prior to entering into the contract, that the client has a net worth of more than $2,700,000.

## Q.3 What the Net Worth Test Excludes

The net worth determination continues to exclude the value of the client's primary residence and certain indebtedness secured by that residence. For a natural person, net worth may include assets held jointly with a spouse. This order does not change either point.

## Q.4 Pre-Existing Contracts Are Preserved

Advisory contracts and private fund subscriptions entered into before 2026-06-29 are generally not subject to the adjusted thresholds. The transition provisions of rule 205-3(c) provide that a person who satisfied the applicable test in effect at the time of admission continues to satisfy it.

An investor admitted under the $2,200,000 net worth test therefore does not re-qualify at $2,700,000. A person admitted on or after the effective date is tested at the adjusted amounts.

## Q.5 Scope

Section 205(a)(1) and rule 205-3 apply to investment advisers registered with the Commission. The qualified client standard is separately incorporated by reference in the law of a number of states. For a private fund relying on section 3(c)(1) of the Investment Company Act, each investor must satisfy the qualified client test.

## Q.6 Relationship to Other Investor Qualification Standards

This order adjusts the rule 205-3 tests only. It does not adjust the accredited investor criteria and it does not adjust the qualified purchaser definition under the Investment Company Act. Those standards are unchanged by this order and continue to apply according to their own terms.
