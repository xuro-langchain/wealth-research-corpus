# SEC Order IA-7104 — Order Approving Adjustment for Inflation of the Dollar Amount Tests in Rule 205-3

Issued 2026-04. Effective 2026-06-29.

## Q.1 Basis for the Order

Section 205(e) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 directs the Commission to adjust for inflation the dollar amount thresholds of the tests set forth in rule 205-3, by order issued on or about May 1 of every fifth year, rounded to the nearest multiple of $100,000, by reference to the Personal Consumption Expenditures Chain-Type Price Index published by the Department of Commerce.

## Q.2 Adjusted Dollar Amount Tests

It is hereby ordered that, effective 2026-06-29, the dollar amount of the assets-under-management test set forth in rule 205-3(d)(1)(i) is $1,400,000, and the dollar amount of the net worth test set forth in rule 205-3(d)(1)(ii) is $2,700,000.

A client is a qualified client if the client has at least $1,400,000 under the management of the investment adviser immediately after entering into the advisory contract, or if the investment adviser reasonably believes, immediately prior to entering into the advisory contract, that the client has a net worth of more than $2,700,000.

## Q.3 Determination of Net Worth

For purposes of the net worth test, net worth is calculated excluding the value of the client's primary residence and any indebtedness secured by that residence up to its fair market value. In the case of a natural person, net worth may include assets held jointly with that person's spouse. This order does not modify either provision.

## Q.4 Contracts Entered Into Before the Effective Date

This order does not apply to an advisory contract entered into, or to an investment in a private fund made, before 2026-06-29. The transition provisions of rule 205-3(c) continue to apply, and a person who satisfied the dollar amount test in effect at the time of that person's entry continues to be a qualified client with respect to that contract or investment.

A determination made before the effective date is preserved and is not disturbed by this order. Such a determination may not be relied upon for a contract entered into, or an investment made, on or after the effective date.

## Q.5 Recordkeeping

An investment adviser relying on a determination under this order shall retain the records required by rule 204-2(a)(8) documenting the basis for that determination, including the dollar amount test relied upon and the date of the determination.

## Q.6 Scope of This Order

This order adjusts the dollar amount tests in rule 205-3 and nothing further. The accredited investor standards of Regulation D are not adjusted by this order. The qualified purchaser definition in section 2(a)(51) of the Investment Company Act of 1940 is not adjusted by this order. Each of those standards continues to apply according to its own terms.
