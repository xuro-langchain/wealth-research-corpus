# SEC Release 2025-08 — Qualified Purchaser Threshold Indexation

Issued 2025-08. Effective for determinations made on or after 2026-01-01. This release adopts amendments indexing the qualified purchaser thresholds and restates the treatment of accredited investor status.

## Q.1 Purpose

This release adopts amendments indexing for inflation the dollar thresholds in the qualified purchaser definition, and restates without change the accredited investor criteria that operate alongside it.

## Q.2 Indexed Thresholds

For determinations made on or after 2026-01-01, the investments threshold for a natural person qualified purchaser is raised to six million two hundred thousand dollars. The threshold for a family company is raised to thirty-one million dollars.

The thresholds will be indexed every five years by the methodology described in the adopting text.

## Q.3 Accredited Investor Status Preserved

The accredited investor criteria are unchanged by this release and continue to apply in full. Nothing in Q.2 disturbs the professional certification pathway adopted previously, and a person qualifying under that pathway remains accredited without regard to the indexed amounts at Q.2.

## Q.4 Determinations Already Made

A determination of qualified purchaser status properly made before 2026-01-01 is not invalidated by this release. A person who ceases to meet the indexed threshold is not required to divest, but may not be treated as a qualified purchaser for a new determination made on or after the effective date.

## Q.5 Recordkeeping

An adviser relying on a determination must retain the basis for it. A determination carried forward from before the effective date must record that it was made under the prior thresholds.
