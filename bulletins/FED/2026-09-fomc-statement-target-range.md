# FED Statement 2026-09-16 — FOMC Raises the Target Range to 3-3/4 to 4 Percent

Issued 2026-09. Effective on publication. Trimmed digest of a real published statement, retained in this corpus for the provisions that bear on duration positioning. Full text: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm

## F.1 Decision

The Committee raised the target range for the federal funds rate by one quarter percentage point to 3-3/4 to 4 percent. The statement was approved by a vote of 12 to 0, with no dissents recorded.

In a related action the Board of Governors approved a quarter point increase in the interest rate paid on reserve balances to 3.90 percent and a quarter point increase in the primary credit rate to 4.0 percent, both effective 2026-09-17.

## F.2 Stated Rationale

The Committee framed the increase as supporting a timelier return to its 2 percent goal, and stated that it will deliver price stability. Inflation remains elevated relative to that goal.

## F.3 Economic Assessment

Economic activity is expanding at a solid pace, with domestic spending holding up despite elevated uncertainty owing in part to geopolitical factors. Productivity growth is described as strong and capital investment as robust. Hiring has kept pace with growth in the workforce and the unemployment rate has changed little.

## F.4 Balance Sheet

The Committee is continuing its policy of maintaining ample reserves in the banking system. No change to the pace of balance sheet reduction is announced in this statement.

## F.5 What This Statement Is Not

This is a decision, not guidance about the path. The statement does not commit the Committee to further increases and does not describe a sequence. A positioning view that reads a path into it is reading something the statement does not contain.

## F.6 Immediately Prior Meeting

At its meeting of 2026-07-28 and 2026-07-29 the Committee maintained the target range at 3-1/2 to 3-3/4 percent by a vote of 9 to 3. The three dissenting members preferred a quarter point increase at that meeting.
