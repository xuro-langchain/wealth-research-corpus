# FED Statement 2026-01 — Policy Rate Path Guidance

Issued 2026-01. Effective on publication. This statement communicates the Committee's assessment and its guidance on the likely path of the target range.

## F.1 Assessment

The Committee judges that inflation has moved closer to the two percent objective and that labour market conditions have come into better balance. Risks to the objectives are judged to be roughly balanced.

## F.2 Guidance on the Path

The Committee anticipates that reductions in the target range will proceed at a measured pace, contingent on continued progress toward the inflation objective. The Committee does not expect it will be appropriate to reduce the target range further until it has gained greater confidence that inflation is moving sustainably toward two percent.

The contingency in this paragraph is not a formality. Participants' projections are conditional on the realised inflation path and are not a commitment.

## F.3 Balance Sheet

The Committee will continue reducing its holdings of Treasury securities and agency mortgage-backed securities at the pace announced previously. No change to that pace is announced in this statement.

## F.4 Data Dependence

The Committee will continue to assess incoming data and the evolving outlook. The Committee would be prepared to adjust the stance of policy as appropriate if risks emerge that could impede the attainment of the objectives.
