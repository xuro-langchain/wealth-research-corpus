Issued 2025-08. Applies to taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01.

## N.1 Purpose

This revenue procedure sets forth the inflation-adjusted alternative minimum tax exemption amounts and the phase-out thresholds applicable to taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01, and supersedes section 2.11 of Rev. Proc. 2024-58 in its entirety.

## N.2 Exemption Amount

For taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01, the alternative minimum tax exemption amount under section 55(d)(1) is $90,100 in the case of an unmarried individual other than a surviving spouse, and $140,200 in the case of married individuals filing a joint return or a surviving spouse. In the case of married individuals filing separately, the exemption amount is one half of the amount applicable to a joint return.

## N.3 Phase-Out Threshold

For taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01, the exemption amount determined under N.2 is reduced by twenty-five cents for each dollar by which the alternative minimum taxable income of the taxpayer exceeds $500,000 in the case of an unmarried individual and $1,000,000 in the case of married individuals filing a joint return.

The thresholds stated in this section are not determined by reference to section 1(f) and are not indexed for taxable years beginning before 2030-01-01. A taxpayer whose alternative minimum taxable income exceeds the applicable threshold is subject to the treatment described in N.4 without regard to whether that taxpayer was subject to the tax under the thresholds previously in effect.

## N.4 Treatment of Interest on Specified Private Activity Bonds

Interest on a specified private activity bond remains an item of tax preference under section 57(a)(5)(A) and is included in alternative minimum taxable income, reduced by any deduction not allowable in computing the regular tax which would have been allowable if such interest were includible in gross income.

For purposes of section 57(a)(5)(C)(i), a specified private activity bond is any private activity bond as defined in section 141 which was issued after 1986-08-07 and the interest on which is not includible in gross income under section 103. This revenue procedure does not modify that definition and does not modify the treatment described in this section.

## N.5 Qualified 501(c)(3) Bonds

The term private activity bond, for purposes of section 57(a)(5)(C)(i), does not include any qualified 501(c)(3) bond as defined in section 145. Interest on a qualified 501(c)(3) bond is not an item of tax preference and is not included in alternative minimum taxable income.

Nothing in N.2 or N.3 applies to interest described in this section. The treatment of interest on a qualified 501(c)(3) bond is preserved in full and is unaffected by the amounts stated in this revenue procedure.

## N.6 Other Excepted Obligations

The exceptions provided by section 57(a)(5)(C)(iii) for certain exempt facility bonds issued for qualified residential rental projects, qualified mortgage bonds and qualified veterans' mortgage bonds continue to apply. The exception provided by section 57(a)(5)(C)(iv) for a refunding bond where the refunded bond, or in a series of refundings the original bond, was issued before 1986-08-08 continues to apply. This revenue procedure does not disturb either exception.

## N.7 Effect on Other Documents

Section 2.11 of Rev. Proc. 2024-58 is superseded. No inference is intended as to the treatment of any item not addressed in this revenue procedure.

## N.8 Effective Date

This revenue procedure applies to taxable years beginning on or after 2026-01-01.
