# IRS Notice 2026-18 — AMT Treatment of Private Activity Bond Interest

Issued 2026-03. Effective for taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01. This notice states the Service's position on the alternative minimum tax treatment of interest on qualified private activity bonds and announces the exemption and phase-out amounts that will apply.

## N.1 Purpose

This notice announces the alternative minimum tax exemption amount and phase-out threshold for taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01, and restates the treatment of interest on qualified private activity bonds issued after 1986 as an item of tax preference under section 57(a)(5).

## N.2 Exemption Amount and Phase-Out Threshold

For taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01, the alternative minimum tax exemption amount is reduced to eighty-five thousand seven hundred dollars for a married individual filing jointly and to fifty-five thousand one hundred dollars for an unmarried individual.

The phase-out threshold is reduced to five hundred thousand dollars for a married individual filing jointly and to three hundred thousand dollars for an unmarried individual. The exemption is reduced by twenty-five cents for each dollar of alternative minimum taxable income above the applicable threshold and is fully phased out at the amounts stated in the table published with this notice.

These amounts are not indexed for taxable years beginning before 2030-01-01.

## N.3 Effect on Private Activity Bond Interest

Interest on a qualified private activity bond issued after 1986 that is not a qualified 501(c)(3) bond remains an item of tax preference and is included in alternative minimum taxable income under section 57(a)(5). This notice does not change that treatment.

The reduction in the exemption amount and the phase-out threshold at N.2 does, however, change the population of taxpayers for whom the preference item produces an actual alternative minimum tax liability. A taxpayer whose alternative minimum taxable income exceeds the reduced threshold at N.2 will be subject to the preference treatment of that interest notwithstanding that the same taxpayer was outside the alternative minimum tax under the amounts previously in effect.

## N.4 Qualified 501(c)(3) Bonds

Interest on a qualified 501(c)(3) bond is not an item of tax preference under section 57(a)(5) and is not affected by this notice. The treatment of interest on such bonds is preserved in full, and nothing in N.2 or N.3 disturbs it.

This distinction is material: obligations of non-profit hospital systems and private higher education institutions are commonly issued as qualified 501(c)(3) bonds, while airport special facility obligations commonly are not.

## N.5 Transition

No transition relief is provided for obligations acquired before the effective date. The treatment at N.3 applies to interest received in a taxable year beginning on or after 2027-01-01 regardless of when the obligation was acquired.

## N.6 Reliance

A taxpayer may rely on the amounts stated at N.2 for taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01 until superseded. The Service intends to publish the indexation methodology for taxable years beginning on or after 2030-01-01 in separate guidance.
