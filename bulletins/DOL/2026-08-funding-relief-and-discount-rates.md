# DOL Release 2026-31 — Pension Funding Relief and Discount Rate Corridor

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** Written for a demonstration corpus. The issuing body, release
> number, dates and dollar amounts are invented. Statutory citations are to real provisions

Issued 2026-08. Effective for plan years beginning on or after 2027-01-01.

## P.1 Purpose

This release adopts amendments to the funding rules applicable to single-employer defined benefit plans, widening the discount rate corridor used to measure funding target liabilities and providing conditional relief from minimum required contributions for plans in surplus.

## P.2 Discount Rate Corridor

For plan years beginning on or after 2027-01-01, the permitted corridor around the twenty-five year average segment rates is widened to twenty percent in each direction, from the ten percent corridor previously in effect.

A wider corridor permits a sponsor to measure liabilities using a higher discount rate. The measured value of plan liabilities falls accordingly, and a plan measured at or near full funding under the prior corridor will in most cases be measured in surplus under this one.

## P.3 Relief From Minimum Required Contributions

A plan whose funded status measured under P.2 exceeds one hundred and ten percent is not required to make a minimum contribution for that plan year. The sponsor may elect to treat the excess as available for the purposes described at P.4.

## P.4 Permitted Uses of a Surplus

A sponsor of a plan meeting the threshold at P.3 may transfer excess assets to a qualified replacement plan, or may apply them to retiree health accounts, subject to the notice requirements at P.6. This release does not permit a reversion to the sponsor outside the existing statutory framework.

## P.5 What Is Not Changed

The benefit accrual and vesting rules are unchanged. The requirements applicable to plans below eighty percent funded status are preserved in full, and nothing in P.2 or P.3 relieves such a plan of any restriction currently applicable to it.

The fiduciary standards governing investment of plan assets are not addressed by this release and continue to apply according to their own terms.

## P.6 Notice

A sponsor electing relief under P.3 shall notify participants within thirty days of the election, in the form prescribed in the appendix to this release.
