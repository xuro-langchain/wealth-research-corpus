## S.1 Summary View

The desk is overweight United States semiconductor capital equipment on a twelve to eighteen month view. The cycle is early, order books are lengthening, and the customer concentration that has historically made this a low-quality cyclical has moderated.

## S.2 Cycle Position

Wafer fab equipment spending troughed in the fourth quarter of 2024 on the desk's reading and has risen for two consecutive quarters. The historical pattern from trough is seven to nine quarters of expansion, which places the peak in late 2026.

Lead times on lithography and deposition tooling have extended past fifty weeks. Extended lead times at this point in the cycle have been a reliable forward indicator in each of the last three cycles.

## S.3 Customer Concentration

The top three customers account for fifty-one percent of segment revenue, down from sixty-four percent at the prior peak. The desk regards this as a genuine quality improvement rather than a mix artefact, driven by the entry of two new logic foundry customers at leading edge.

## S.4 What Would Change the View

A capex guidance reduction from either of the two largest logic customers. A sustained decline in memory pricing below cash cost, which historically precedes memory capex deferral by two quarters. Any export control action materially restricting shipments to the customer base.

## S.5 Positioning

Express through capital equipment rather than through the fabless or memory segments, where the desk holds no view at this edition.
