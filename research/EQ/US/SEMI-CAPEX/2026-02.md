# US Semiconductor Capital Expenditure Cycle (Note EQ-US-SEMI-CAPEX, Edition 2026-02)

Published 2026-02-19 by US Equity Research. Applies to positions taken on or after 2026-03-01. This edition supersedes edition 2025-06.

## S.1 Summary View

The desk reduces United States semiconductor capital equipment to neutral from overweight. The cycle thesis at S.2 of edition 2025-06 has largely played out; the desk is taking the position down on valuation and cycle position, not on a deterioration in fundamentals.

## S.2 Cycle Position

Wafer fab equipment spending has now risen for six consecutive quarters. On the seven to nine quarter pattern described in edition 2025-06 at S.2, the expansion is two to three quarters from its peak.

Lead times have stopped extending and have shortened modestly on deposition tooling. The desk reads the reversal as the ordinary late-cycle signal it has been in prior cycles rather than as a demand event.

## S.3 Customer Concentration

Concentration has improved further, to forty-six percent for the top three customers. The structural argument at S.3 of edition 2025-06 is preserved in full and is not the reason for the downgrade.

## S.4 Power Constraint

A constraint absent from the prior edition now binds: leading-edge fab siting is increasingly gated by grid interconnection timelines rather than by tooling availability. The desk treats this as capping the rate of capacity addition rather than the level, and it is covered in EQ-GL-AI-INFRA-POWER 2026-01 at P.4.

## S.5 Positioning

Neutral weight. The desk retains a preference for lithography over deposition and etch within the segment on pricing power grounds, which is unchanged from the prior edition.
