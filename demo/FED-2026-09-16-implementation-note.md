# FED Implementation Note 2026-09-16 — Rates on Reserve Balances and Primary Credit

Issued 2026-09. Effective 2026-09-17. Trimmed digest of a real published implementation note, staged for ingest during the demo. Net-new; it implements a decision already in the corpus and supersedes nothing. Full text: https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a1.htm

## I.1 Purpose

This note records the administered rate settings that implement the target range decision announced in the Committee's statement of the same date. It is a mechanical companion to that statement and states no view of its own.

## I.2 Interest on Reserve Balances

The Board of Governors voted unanimously to raise the interest rate paid on reserve balances to 3.90 percent, effective 2026-09-17.

## I.3 Primary Credit Rate

The Board voted unanimously to approve a one quarter percentage point increase in the primary credit rate to 4.0 percent, effective 2026-09-17. In taking this action the Board approved requests submitted by the boards of directors of the Federal Reserve Banks of Cleveland, Richmond, Atlanta, Chicago, Minneapolis, Kansas City and Dallas.

## I.4 Relationship to the Statement

This note implements the target range of 3-3/4 to 4 percent announced at F.1 of the Committee's statement of 2026-09-16. It does not modify that statement, does not extend it, and carries no guidance about the path beyond what F.5 of the statement already says.

## I.5 Open Market Operations

No change is announced to the pace of balance sheet reduction. The Committee's policy of maintaining ample reserves in the banking system, stated at F.4 of the statement, continues to apply in full.
