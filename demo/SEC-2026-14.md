# SEC Release 2026-14 — Qualified Purchaser Exemption Relief for Registered Advisers

Issued 2026-05. Effective for determinations made on or after 2026-07-01. This release provides limited relief from the qualified purchaser threshold for certain advisory clients.

## E.1 Purpose

This release provides an exemption from the indexed investments threshold at Q.2 of Release 2025-08 for a natural person who is a client of a registered investment adviser and who meets the conditions at E.2.

## E.2 Conditions

The exemption is available where the person has been an advisory client of the registered adviser for at least twenty-four consecutive months, the adviser exercises discretionary authority over at least four million dollars of the person's investments, and the adviser has documented a suitability determination specific to the private markets allocation proposed.

All three conditions must be satisfied. The exemption is not available on the basis of the investments test alone.

## E.3 Effect

A person meeting the conditions at E.2 may be treated as a qualified purchaser notwithstanding that the person does not meet the indexed investments threshold at Q.2 of Release 2025-08. This restores access for a population that the indexation at Q.2 removed.

## E.4 What Is Not Changed

The family company threshold at Q.2 of Release 2025-08 is preserved in full and no relief is provided from it.

The accredited investor criteria are unchanged and continue to apply. This release does not provide relief from any requirement other than the natural person investments threshold.

## E.5 Recordkeeping

An adviser relying on this exemption must retain the documentation of each condition at E.2, including the suitability determination, for the period the client's private markets allocation is held and for five years thereafter.
