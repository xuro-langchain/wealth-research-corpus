# US Municipal Credit Outlook (Note FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT, Edition 2026-04)

Published 2026-04-09 by US Fixed Income Research. Applies to positions taken on or after 2026-05-01. Re-issued out of cycle in response to IRS Notice 2026-18, under the review trigger at M.8 of edition 2025-11.

## M.1 Summary View

The desk moves United States municipal credit to neutral from overweight. The private activity concentration recommended in edition 2025-11 is withdrawn and is replaced by a narrower preference limited to qualified 501(c)(3) obligations.

This supersedes edition 2025-11 in its entirety for positions taken on or after 2026-05-01. Edition 2025-11 remains the basis of record for positions taken while it stood.

## M.2 Why the Prior View Does Not Survive

Edition 2025-11 rested at M.2 on an after-tax advantage available to top-bracket holders who fell outside the alternative minimum tax under the exemption and phase-out amounts then in force. That note stated the assumption as load-bearing and stated at M.7 that a change to the AMT exemption amount or phase-out threshold would invalidate the recommendation.

IRS Notice 2026-18 at N.2 reduces the exemption amount and the phase-out threshold for taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01. At N.3 the Service confirms that the preference treatment of post-1986 private activity interest is unchanged, but that the reduced threshold draws into the alternative minimum tax taxpayers who were previously outside it.

On the desk's modelling of the reduced amounts at N.2, the majority of the top-bracket client base that the prior note assumed to be outside the alternative minimum tax now falls inside it for taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01. The after-tax advantage that justified the overweight compresses to approximately fifteen basis points on the desk's central case, from the ninety to one hundred and ten basis points assumed in edition 2025-11. That is inside transaction costs for the segment.

The overweight therefore does not survive, and the desk withdraws it rather than re-justifying it on the fundamental case at M.3 of the prior edition, which the prior edition itself stated was insufficient to support the position size.

## M.3 What Is Preserved

IRS Notice 2026-18 at N.4 expressly preserves the treatment of interest on qualified 501(c)(3) bonds, which are not items of tax preference under section 57(a)(5) and are unaffected by the reduced amounts at N.2.

Two of the three preferred sectors in edition 2025-11 at M.5 — non-profit hospital systems and private higher education — are predominantly issued as qualified 501(c)(3) obligations. For those sectors the after-tax case is intact and the desk retains a modest preference.

The third preferred sector at M.5, airport special facility paper, is predominantly non-501(c)(3) private activity issuance. That preference is withdrawn in full.

## M.4 Revised Positioning

Municipal credit to neutral weight. Within the municipal allocation the desk prefers qualified 501(c)(3) obligations of non-profit hospital systems and private higher education, on the fundamental criteria stated at M.5 of edition 2025-11, which are unchanged.

The desk recommends no new positions in non-501(c)(3) private activity paper. Existing positions are not recommended for forced sale: the treatment at N.3 applies to interest received in taxable years beginning on or after 2027-01-01, and an orderly reduction ahead of that date will realise better execution than a compressed one.

## M.5 Supply

The supply picture described at M.4 of edition 2025-11 is unchanged and remains a genuine technical support. It was never sufficient on its own to justify the overweight and it is not sufficient now.

## M.6 Duration and Curve

Unchanged from edition 2025-11 at M.6. The eight to fifteen year band remains the desk's preference and the front end remains unattractive.

## M.7 Risks to the View

The Service has stated at N.6 that it intends to publish an indexation methodology for taxable years beginning on or after 2030-01-01. A methodology that restores the exemption amount in real terms would re-open the question this note closes.

A change in the top marginal federal rate continues to scale the after-tax advantage in both directions.

Legislative reversal of the amounts at N.2 before the 2027-01-01 effective date, which the desk does not regard as the base case.

## M.8 Review Trigger

Reviewed on the earlier of its twelve-month anniversary, publication of the indexation methodology described at N.6, or any announced change to the federal tax treatment of municipal interest.
