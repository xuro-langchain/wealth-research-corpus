# Private Markets Eligibility Determination Guide

Internal compliance guidance on determining and documenting client eligibility for private markets strategies. This is not a research note and not investment advice to any client. It states who may be offered these strategies; it does not state whether they should be.

Last revised 2026-01-20 by Compliance.

## P.1 Standing

No private markets strategy may be offered to a client until eligibility is determined and documented under this guide. Eligibility is a regulatory determination and is not within portfolio manager discretion at any authority level.

## P.2 Qualified Purchaser Determination

A natural person is a qualified purchaser where the person meets the indexed investments threshold stated at Q.2 of SEC Release 2025-08, as determined on or after the effective date stated in that release.

This guide implements the thresholds at Q.2 as required by that release. It does not restate them, because a restated threshold is a threshold that will eventually be wrong; read the release.

## P.3 Determinations Carried Forward

A determination properly made before the effective date of SEC Release 2025-08 remains valid under Q.4 of that release. The file must record that the determination was made under the prior thresholds, per Q.5.

A carried-forward determination may not be used as the basis for a new commitment made on or after the effective date.

## P.4 Accredited Investor Status

Accredited investor status is determined separately and is unchanged by SEC Release 2025-08, which preserves those criteria in full at Q.3. A client may be accredited without being a qualified purchaser, and the strategies available differ accordingly.

## P.5 Documentation

Every determination records the pathway relied on, the evidence, the person making the determination, and the date. A determination without a recorded basis is treated as no determination in audit.

## P.6 Suitability Is Separate

Eligibility is necessary and not sufficient. An eligible client must still satisfy the suitability standard and the liquidity constraint at V.6 of MA-GL-PRIVATE-MARKETS 2025-12 before a commitment is made.

A file that records eligibility and omits suitability is incomplete, and this is the most common deficiency found in review.
