# US Taxable Account Fixed Income Allocation Guide

Internal investment guidance setting the fixed income sleeve weights for United States taxable client accounts. This is not a research note and not investment advice to any client. It translates the research desk's published views into binding portfolio weights, and it may never assert a view the research corpus does not support.

Last revised 2025-07-15 by the Investment Policy Committee.

## A.1 Standing

This guide governs what a portfolio manager may hold without escalation. Where it states a weight, that weight is the default and a deviation beyond the tolerance band in A.6 requires approval under the discretion matrix at D.3.

A weight in this guide is only as current as the research note it rests on. Where a note this guide cites has been superseded or withdrawn, the weight derived from it is suspended — not carried forward — until the committee re-adopts it against the replacement note. A portfolio manager who finds a cited note superseded must treat the corresponding weight as requiring escalation under D.4.

## A.2 Sleeve Composition

The taxable fixed income sleeve is composed of United States Treasuries, agency mortgage-backed securities, investment grade corporate credit, and municipal credit. Non-US developed sovereign and emerging market debt are held in the multi-asset sleeve and are governed by the global bands guide, not this document.

## A.3 Municipal Weight

For clients in the top federal marginal bracket, municipal credit carries a target weight of twenty-two percent of the fixed income sleeve, against a neutral weight of eighteen percent.

The four point overweight, and its concentration in the private activity segment, is adopted from FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT edition 2025-06 at M.1, and rests on the after-tax analysis at M.2 of that note. The committee adopted the desk's own framing: this is an after-tax position, not a credit position, and the fundamental case at M.3 supports only a portion of it.

For clients below the top federal bracket, the municipal weight is the neutral eighteen percent and the private activity concentration does not apply. The after-tax advantage that justifies the overweight is a top-bracket advantage and does not exist for these accounts.

## A.4 Municipal Sector Limits

Within the municipal allocation, the sector preferences at M.5 of FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT 2025-06 are binding as limits and not merely as preferences.

No more than thirty-five percent of the municipal allocation may sit in any one of the preferred sectors named at M.5. No position may be taken in the sectors the desk is underweight at M.5 — standalone senior living, single-asset student housing, and single-obligor industrial development paper — without approval under D.3.

Municipal duration is held in the eight to fifteen year band, per M.6. Extension beyond fifteen years requires approval and may not be granted on a portfolio-wide basis.

## A.5 Investment Grade Corporate Weight

Investment grade corporate credit carries a target weight of twenty-eight percent against a neutral of thirty-two percent. The four point underweight is the funding source for the municipal overweight at A.3 and moves with it: if the municipal overweight is suspended under A.1, the corporate underweight is suspended with it and both return to neutral.

This pairing is deliberate. A suspended municipal overweight that left the corporate underweight in place would leave the sleeve structurally short credit for no stated reason.

## A.6 Tolerance Bands

Each target weight carries a tolerance band of plus or minus two percentage points, measured at month end on market value. Drift beyond the band is rebalanced at the next monthly cycle.

A band breach caused by a suspension under A.1 is not a drift breach and is not rebalanced automatically. It requires the escalation at D.4, because the correct weight is a committee decision and not a mechanical calculation.

## A.7 Tax-Loss Harvesting Interaction

Harvesting within the municipal allocation must respect the sector limits at A.4 on the replacement position, not merely on the position sold. A harvest that concentrates the replacement in a single preferred sector above the A.4 limit is a limit breach on the day it settles.

## A.8 Review

This guide is reviewed quarterly, and out of cycle whenever a research note it cites is re-issued or withdrawn. The notes it currently rests on are FI-US-MUNI-CREDIT 2025-06 and FI-US-IG-SPREADS 2025-09.
