## G.1 Summary View

The desk is underweight United States investment grade corporate credit. Spreads are inside the tenth percentile of their twenty-year range and the desk sees limited compensation for the cyclical risk carried.

## G.2 Valuation

Index spread stands near the tightest levels since 2007 on an option-adjusted basis. Adjusting for the index's improved average rating and shorter average duration narrows but does not eliminate the richness.

## G.3 Fundamentals

Leverage is stable and interest coverage remains comfortable at the index level. The desk's concern is valuation and not credit deterioration, and the underweight should be understood accordingly.

## G.4 Technicals

Demand from liability-driven investors has been the dominant technical for six quarters and shows no sign of reversing. This is the principal argument against the underweight and the desk gives it real weight.

## G.5 Positioning

Underweight by approximately four percentage points within a taxable fixed income sleeve, with the proceeds available to fund a higher-conviction position elsewhere in the sleeve rather than held in cash.

## G.6 Risks

A continuation of the technical at G.4 beyond the desk's expectation. A growth acceleration that validates current spreads. The underweight is a carry-negative position and a prolonged range-bound market costs money.
